# Credit Risk Intelligence - Lending Club

> Predicting Probability of Default (PD) and Lending Portfolio Optimization

---

## 1. Overview

This project develops a credit risk assessment system using LendingClub data, featuring:

- **PD Prediction Model:** Built with LightGBM and fine-tuned using Optuna.
- **Probability Calibration:** Utilizing Platt Scaling to ensure the PD outputs are reliable and reflect real-world default frequencies.
- **4 Optimization Scenarios:** Designed to support strategic business decision-making.
- **Streamlit Dashboard:** Providing full visualization of all results and model performance.

## 2. Lending Club
LendingClub is an American fintech company operating in the online consumer lending sector. Initially, the company operated under a peer-to-peer (P2P) lending model—directly connecting borrowers with investors through a digital platform.
Over time, LendingClub evolved into a hybrid lending platform (combining a marketplace with a balance sheet model), in which the company simultaneously:
- Connects investors with borrowers.
- Directly participates in the credit extension process.

The core of LendingClub’s operations is not merely "lending," but rather: **Data-driven credit decisioning.**

## 3. Decision Simulation and Optimization
### 3.1 Scenario 1: Optimal Interest Rate (Individual Level)
In this scenario, the group holds the customer profile constant and tests various interest rate levels. For each rate, the model re-estimates the Probability of Default (PD) to calculate the Expected Profit.
- Goal: Determine the optimal interest rate that maximizes expected profit.
- Business Question: "What specific interest rate should be offered to this particular customer?"
- Strength: It captures systemic endogeneity—the reality that as interest rates rise, the probability of default also tends to increase.
- Limitation: The current model assumes a 100% take-up rate (customers accept any rate). In reality, higher rates decrease the likelihood of a customer accepting the loan.

### 3.2 Scenario 2: Risk-Based Pricing (Segment Level)
This scenario scales the individual approach up to customer segments. The group simulates various interest rates for different risk groups (sub_grades) to select the optimal rate for each.
Outcome: A risk-based pricing table, similar to how financial institutions establish pricing policies.
- Key Insight: Profit does not increase linearly with interest rates. If rates are too high, the spike in default probability offsets the interest gains, reducing overall profit.
- Improvement: Move away from legacy "sub_grades" and instead use risk clusters based on the PD generated by the group's own model to ensure independence.

### 3.3 Scenario 3: Approval Policy Optimization (Portfolio Level)
At the portfolio level, the group simulates different approval thresholds based on default probabilities. Loans with a PD lower than the threshold are approved, and the total portfolio profit is calculated.
- Goal: Identify the "sweet spot" that balances business expansion with risk control.
- Business Question: "What is the maximum level of risk we should accept?"
- Findings:
      + Approve all: Low profit due to high default losses.
      + Too conservative: Low profit due to missed revenue opportunities.
- The Result: An optimal threshold exists between these two extremes.

*Note: While the model assumes loans are independent (ignoring macroeconomic cycles), this remains a valid simplification for the scope of this research.*

### 3.4 Scenario 4: Capital Allocation Optimization
The group addresses the challenge of allocating limited capital across different customer segments to maximize profit while constraining risk (e.g., using Value-at-Risk).
- Context: This is a portfolio management problem, mirroring how financial institutions manage lending books.
- Business Question: "How should capital be distributed across customer groups to achieve the highest efficiency?"

## 5. Pipeline Flow

```
lending_club_loan_cleaned.csv
    ↓ Feature Engineering
    ↓ Encode & Train/Test Split (80/20, stratified)
    ↓ Baseline: LightGBM / XGBoost / LogReg × Full / Top15
    ↓ Fine-tune LightGBM (Optuna, 20 trials)
    ↓ Evaluate (AUC, KS, Gini, F2)
    ↓ Calibration (Platt Scaling)
    ↓ Predict PD
    ├── KB1: Optimal Interest Rate (1 khách hàng)
    ├── KB2: Pricing Policy theo Sub_Grade
    ├── KB3: Portfolio Approval Policy
    └── KB4: Capital Allocation Risk/Return
    ↓ Save Artifacts → Streamlit
```
